Find your problem below. Most of them have a short fix.

## Who uses this

Everyone who signs in.

## Before you start

Reload the page once and check your internet connection. Many problems are gone after that.

## Step by step

### I cannot sign in

1. Check the email and the password. The email must be the one registered for you. See [[sign-in]].
2. If it is your first time, enter your email and a password of at least 6 characters, sign in, then follow the email you receive.
3. If you forgot the password, request a reset link as described in [[sign-in]].
4. If it says `Your account is inactive.`, contact the RPMO team.

### I cannot find my ticket

1. A ticket you just sent is not in the list until Eclectic approves it. Check `Submission History` from the `Tickets` page.
2. Clear the filters on the `Tickets` page, and check the scope switch. See [[track-tickets]].
3. A member sees only their own tickets and those of their contact group. A company admin sees all.

### My reply or file did not go through

1. Keep replies under 5,000 characters, with at most 5 files of 10 MB. See [[ticket-chat]].
2. When you create a ticket, you can attach up to 10 files of 20 MB each.
3. If the portal says `Your session has expired`, sign in again and resend.

### I get no notifications

1. Open `Settings` › `Notifications` and check `Enable Notifications`. See [[notifications]].
2. Click on the page once, because browsers block sound before any interaction.

## What happens next

If none of this helps, contact the helpdesk team and give the ticket number, the time, and the message you saw.

## Common questions

**The page looks broken.** Reload with Ctrl and F5. If it persists, try another browser.

**I see `Too many requests`.** Wait a minute, then try again.
