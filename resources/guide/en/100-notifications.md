The bell at the top right tells you when something changed in your tickets, so you do not have to keep checking.

## Who uses this

Everyone who signs in.

## Before you start

- Notifications must be switched on in `Settings`. See [[settings]].
- The portal checks for new notifications every 30 seconds while a page is open. The sound plays and the badge on the bell updates when a new one arrives.

## Step by step

1. Look at the bell at the top right. A badge with a number means unread notifications.
2. Select the bell to open the list. Unread items have a pink background.
3. Select an item to open the ticket it is about. It is then marked as read.
4. Select `Mark all read` to clear the badge in one step.
5. To change or switch off the sound, open `Settings` › `Notifications`.

## What happens next

You get a notification when a helpdesk agent replies to your ticket or changes its status. Open the ticket to read the reply. See [[ticket-chat]].

## Common questions

**I hear no sound.** Check `Enable Notifications` and `Notification Sound` in `Settings`. Your browser may also block sound until you click on the page once.

**The number does not change right away.** The portal checks every 30 seconds. Reload the page to check at once.

**How do I clear the history?** In `Settings` › `Notifications`, select `Clear All` under `Clear Notification History`. It marks everything as read and clears the badge.
