JARVIES is the support portal of Eclectic. Customers use it to send support requests, follow them, and talk to the support team in one place.

## Who uses this

- **Customers:** contacts of a company that has a support agreement. You see only your own company's tickets.
- **Super Admin:** Eclectic staff who work on tickets from the portal.

This guide shows only the topics that apply to your account. If you are a customer, topics for staff do not appear.

## Before you start

- You need an account. If your email is registered as a contact of your company, you can set up your own password the first time you sign in. See [[sign-in]].
- Use a current browser. The portal works on desktop and on a phone.

## Step by step

The menu on the left has everything you need:

1. `Dashboard` shows the numbers and the latest tickets. See [[dashboard]].
2. `Tickets` lists every ticket you can see, and is where you start a new one. See [[track-tickets]].
3. `Settings` changes how the portal looks and sounds. See [[settings]].
4. `User Guide` opens this guide.

At the top right, the bell shows new updates, and your name opens `Profile`, `Settings` and sign out. See [[notifications]].

## What happens next

When you send a ticket, Eclectic reviews it first, then assigns it to a consultant. You are notified when something changes, and you can reply in the ticket at any time. When your issue is solved, you close the ticket.

## Common questions

**Who can see my tickets?** People of your own company, depending on their access. See [[track-tickets]].

**Where do I get help with the portal itself?** See [[troubleshooting]]. If it does not solve the problem, contact the helpdesk or the RPMO team.
